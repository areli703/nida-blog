# SiteSwan vs Vendasta (2026): Which White-Label Platform Actually Makes Resellers Money?

If you’re researching the best white-label website reseller program, you’ve probably landed on two names: **SiteSwan** and **Vendasta**. On the surface, both let you sell websites under your own brand. Under the hood, they are completely different animals.

One is a website-focused reseller kit with a flat monthly fee. The other is a massive marketplace of digital products wrapped inside a platform. We signed up for both, reviewed the contracts, and ran the math on what each actually costs a solo reseller trying to turn a profit.

Here is the honest, head-to-head comparison.

---

## What You’re Actually Buying

**SiteSwan** is a white-label website builder and reseller program. You get a library of industry templates, a client dashboard, hosting, and a flat monthly price that lets you build unlimited sites. It is built for one thing: websites for local businesses.

**Vendasta** is a digital marketplace and fulfillment platform. Websites are just one of dozens of products you can resell—alongside reputation management, social posting, SEO, and advertising. Vendasta is built for agencies that want to offer a buffet of services without building them in-house.

Think of it this way: SiteSwan is a specialty store. Vendasta is a department store.

---

## Pricing: What Each Platform Really Costs

Sticker prices rarely tell the full story. Resellers need to know what comes out of their pocket before they ever invoice a client.

| Cost Layer | SiteSwan | Vendasta |
|---|---|---|
| **Platform fee** | $149–$299/mo (unlimited sites) | $0–$999+/mo (varies by package tier) |
| **Setup or onboarding fee** | Usually included | Sometimes required at higher tiers |
| **Per-site cost** | $0 | Included in bundle, or extra for custom builds |
| **Hosting** | Included | Included (sometimes white-labeled, sometimes not) |
| **CRM / automation** | Not included | Included in higher-tier bundles |
| **White-label depth** | Full branding | Varies by product; some vendor branding leaks through |
| **Minimum contract** | None | Sometimes 12 months at higher tiers |

**The hidden difference:** SiteSwan pricing is flat. You pay one number and build as many sites as you can sell. Vendasta pricing scales with the bundle you choose, and the cheapest tiers often lack the white-label infrastructure serious resellers need. If you only want websites, you may be subsidizing a dozen tools you never use.

---

## White-Label Depth: Whose Brand Does the Client See?

White-label isn’t binary. A platform can let you swap a logo but still expose the underlying vendor through login URLs, emails, or invoices.

| Branding Element | SiteSwan | Vendasta |
|---|---|---|
| **Logo / colors** | Full customization | Full customization |
| **Client login URL** | Custom domain capable | Often Vendasta subdomain unless upgraded |
| **Email notifications** | From your brand | May show Vendasta origin |
| **Billing (to your client)** | You invoice directly | You invoice, but fulfillment reveals third parties |
| **Product feel** | Single-purpose; consistent | Multi-vendor; quality and branding vary by product |

SiteSwan wins on consistency. Every client sees the same dashboard, built by the same team, under your brand. Vendasta wins on breadth—your client gets a full suite—but the trade-off is that some products inside the bundle are clearly made by outside vendors.

If your client is even slightly technical, they may figure out they’re sitting inside a marketplace. That erodes trust.

---

## Fulfillment Speed: How Long Until Your Client Is Live?

Speed matters. The faster you deliver, the faster you get paid, the faster you ask for referrals.

| Task | SiteSwan | Vendasta |
|---|---|---|
| **First site build** | 2–4 hours (template-based) | 3–8 hours (template or outsourced) |
| **Customizations** | Built-in editor, moderate flexibility | Depends on product; some require support tickets |
| **Client onboarding** | Simple dashboard walkthrough | More complex due to multi-product options |
| **Ongoing edits** | You make edits directly | You or Vendasta fulfillment team |
| **Support turnaround** | Generally 24–48 hours | Varies widely by product line |

SiteSwan is faster for pure website delivery because it is narrower. Vendasta can be fast if you stick to templated websites, but resellers who drift into bundled SEO or ad fulfillment often hit delays because those products are fulfilled by outside teams.

## Profit Margins: A Real Reseller Scenario

Let’s run the same hypothetical business through both platforms.

**The scenario:** You sign 10 local business clients in your first quarter. Each client pays you $150/month for a website + basic hosting + minor edits.

| Cost / Revenue | SiteSwan | Vendasta |
|---|---|---|
| **Monthly platform cost** | $149–$299 | $299–$699 (package with website + CRM + automations) |
| **Revenue (10 clients × $150)** | $1,500 | $1,500 |
| **Gross profit** | $1,201–$1,351 | $801–$1,201 |
| **Annual profit at 10 clients** | ~$14,400–$16,200 | ~$9,600–$14,400 |

The margin gap widens as you scale. At 20 clients, SiteSwan’s flat fee stays the same. Vendasta’s pricing often climbs as you add products or seats. The reseller paying a flat $149/month keeps significantly more of every client dollar than the one paying $499+ for a bundled marketplace.

**When Vendasta makes sense:** If your clients genuinely need reputation management, listing services, ad fulfillment, and websites—and you want one bill from one vendor—bundling can save administrative time. That time savings has value. Just know you are paying for it.

## Honest Drawbacks: What Neither Platform Talks About

**SiteSwan’s gaps:**
- No built-in CRM or automation. If you want follow-up sequences, appointment booking, or email campaigns, you will need a second tool.
- No true SaaS revenue. You are selling websites and hosting, not software subscriptions. That means your clients could technically leave if they find a cheaper web designer.
- Template reliance. Highly custom designs require workarounds.

**Vendasta’s gaps:**
- Bundle bloat. You pay for a toolkit even if you only need websites.
- Variable white-label depth. Some products inside the bundle expose vendors.
- Wholesale margin pressure. Because you buy at wholesale and mark up, aggressive pricing competition can squeeze your margins.
- Complexity. New resellers often spend weeks learning the platform before they ever pitch a client.

## Who Should Choose What

| Choose SiteSwan if… | Choose Vendasta if… |
|---|---|
| You want the fastest path from signup to first client | You plan to sell a full suite (SEO, ads, reviews, websites) |
| You care more about margin than product breadth | You have staff to manage fulfillment complexity |
| Your clients need simple local business websites | Your clients are mid-market and need enterprise-grade tools |
| You want a flat, predictable monthly cost | You prefer wholesale pricing and want to set your own margins |
| You’re a solo operator without time to learn a massive platform | You want a marketplace to white-label products you don’t build |

---

## The Third Option Most Resellers Ignore

SiteSwan and Vendasta both assume you are happy piecing together a tech stack.

SiteSwan gives you websites but no CRM. Vendasta gives you a marketplace but charges you for products you may not need. If what you actually want is a single platform with websites *and* client management *and* automation *and* flat monthly pricing, neither option is built for that natively.

That is why some resellers eventually move to an all-in-one white-label system that combines the simplicity of SiteSwan with the product depth of Vendasta—without the bundle bloat or extra tool subscriptions.

---

## Bottom Line

SiteSwan and Vendasta serve different reseller profiles.

SiteSwan is the better choice if you want a lean, fast, website-focused business with high margins and low overhead. Vendasta is the better choice if you are building a multi-service agency, have the time to master a complex platform, and can absorb higher monthly costs in exchange for product variety.

If your goal is to start selling in days, keep costs flat, and build a recurring revenue base around websites and follow-up automation, the honest answer is that both platforms force compromises.
